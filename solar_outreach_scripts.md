## Compliance (US)
- CAN-SPAM: truthful subject/from, physical address, working opt-out honored within 10 business days.
- Use business emails only (published on the company site or verified by a tool). Don't guess-and-blast.
- Keep a do-not-contact list. Don't text numbers without consent (TCPA).

## Qualification checklist (all must pass before "QUALIFIED")
1. Owner/founder confirmed on the company's own site, state license, or SOS filing (not only directories)
2. Email published by the company or verified by an email verifier (not guessed)
3. Active in last 12 months (recent projects/reviews/news)
4. Owner-operated with roughly 3-100 staff; US-based installer
5. Has a visible pain: manual lead follow-up, status updates, admin load
